How We Work

We work well with students

A lot of our tenants are at UW. We're flexible about what counts as proof of finances, and a student ID works as one of your two forms of ID.

Maintenance is in-house

Text or call and we'll send someone out. Tenants cover issues they cause; we handle everything else.

Turned over properly

Cleaning and landscaping are professionally done between every lease, so you're not inheriting the last tenant's mess.

Clear, fixed terms

Fixed-term leases with straightforward renewals. Deposit and fees add up to one month's rent, and 90% of that comes back to you.

Dense, walkable locations

Most of our buildings are within walking distance of UW, the light rail, or Ravenna Park.

Furnishing available

Several of our properties can come fully furnished at no additional cost — useful if you're moving in from out of state.

Want to see a place?

Tell us which property you're interested in and we'll set up a tour. Local law gives you 1 day's notice for tours and 2 days for maintenance visits.
