What renting with us looks like

The things tenants ask about most, answered up front.

Maintenance we handle ourselves

Text or call our team any time and we'll send a technician out. Tenants cover issues they cause; the landlord covers everything else.

Built for students

We work with a lot of students, so we're flexible about what counts as proof of finances — and a student ID is accepted as one of your two forms of ID.

Professionally turned over

Cleaning and landscaping are professionally done between leases, so you move into a place that's actually been reset — not just vacated.

Pets are welcome

A $500 deposit per pet, capped at 25% of one month's rent. Emotional support animals are accommodated with a letter from a licensed provider.

Renewals you can plan around

We can almost always do a fixed-term renewal on a 12-month term, so you're not guessing about next year in the middle of this one.

Notice before we visit

One day's notice before a tour, two days for maintenance and everything else — the notice local law requires, every time.

Ready to apply?

Our application asks for basic personal info, proof of ID, and proof of finances. We work with a lot of students, so we're flexible about what counts.
